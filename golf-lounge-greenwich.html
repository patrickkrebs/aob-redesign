A private clubhouse under one roof: a golf simulator, a media lounge and a bar, wrapped in dark wood for long Connecticut winters.

The rooms borrow from the gentlemen’s clubs of the city but lose the stuffiness. Deep leather chairs face the screen, the wood is dark enough to make the light feel golden, and the bar is lit from within its shelving so bottles read as a collection.

Images shown are design visualizations.

Dark wood

Paneling dark enough to disappear, so the screen and the people are what you see.

Low light

Lamps and concealed strips instead of overheads, set for evening.

Play

A full simulator bay that still feels like a room, not equipment.
