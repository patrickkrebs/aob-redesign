A compound for summers with everyone home: beamed dining rooms, white stairs and baths that feel like the shore.

The architecture is classic Vineyard, so the interiors stay relaxed and bright. Long tables seat the whole family under exposed beams, a white stair climbs past windows full of water, and the baths are finished in soft, salt-washed tones.

Images shown are design visualizations.

Beams

Exposed timber overhead keeps big rooms feeling like a cottage.

A white stair and painted millwork bounce the island light deep into the house.

Baths in soft, washed tones that feel like the walk back from the beach.
