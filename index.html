The Studio

We make rooms that feel like a long exhale, built with the obsessive custom detail Aubrey learned designing for Disney, for people who actually live in them.

Aubrey O’Brien, Inc. takes on a small number of homes and hotels each year and gives each one a visual language of its own. Materials are chosen for how they age, finishes for how they feel in the hand, and the furniture is built by makers we know by first name.

The Hidden Detail

Every AOB home hides one detail, just for you.

At Disney, Aubrey learned the art of the hidden Mickey: a small, secret flourish that rewards whoever looks closely. She never stopped. Somewhere in every home the studio finishes is a detail drawn only for its owner. It might be a monogram in the millwork, a family date set in stone, or a favorite color inside a drawer. You’ll know where it is. Your guests might not.

There is one hidden on this page, too.

Process

The process

Four phases, with Aubrey as your single point of contact.

01

Discovery

A conversation in your home about how you live, host and rest. We walk the site, read the plans and agree on scope.

2–4 weeks

02

Concept

Plans, a material palette and the full design, with samples you can hold. We refine it together until it’s right.

6–10 weeks

03

Make

Drawings for your builder, custom pieces commissioned, every order placed, tracked and inspected by the studio.

Through construction

04

Install

We install and style everything, down to linens and books. You arrive to a finished house.

1–2 weeks

Aubrey O’Brien · Founder & Principal

Founder

Trained by Imagineers, at home on the coast

Aubrey started out designing the Walt Disney World Resorts under Disney Legend Wing Chao, where every textile, light and chair was drawn from scratch. Then came watches for the Fossil Group, an artist residency in Shanghai, and a deep dive at NYU into how the brain responds to a room.

Now she brings all of it to private homes, from her studio in Downtown Sarasota. Her view is simple: the right room changes how you feel every day.
