Homepage

Redesign concept · October 2026

From a template to a principal’s studio.

Aubrey’s credentials are first rate: Disney under Wing Chao, an Interior Design award, the Wall Street Journal and the St. Regis. The current site presents them like a starter template. This brief explains what changed and where each decision came from.

03 · Design system

Sarasota Modern

Display · Freight Big Pro Light

O’Brien

Headlines, project names and the name itself. A high-contrast face drawn for large sizes, in the same register as RH and Ralph Lauren Home.

Captions · Freight Text Pro Italic

The sunken lounge, gathered around a suspended fireplace.

Captions, credits and notes. Freight’s text cut, so it sits naturally with the display face.

Body & labels · Neue Haas Unica

Materials chosen for how they age.

Body at 17px Light; labels in Medium caps, tracked wide. A refined Helvetica descendant, quieter than a geometric sans.

05 · To launch

What we need from the studio

  1. Original high-resolution photography for St. Regis, the Golf Lounge, Martha’s Vineyard and the Greenwich home. The current site only has 650–1,000px screenshots of these, many of them renderings.
  2. Two or three client testimonials, approved for use. The site has none today.
  3. Press links and dates for the WSJ, LUXE, Interior Design and Florida Design features, plus magazine covers if available.
  4. Project facts: year, size, scope, architect, builder and photographer for each case study.
  5. Sign-off on proposed copy: service descriptions, process phases and durations, budget ranges in the inquiry form, Journal topics and the “hidden detail” promise are drafts. Confirm she wants to make that promise before it goes live.
  6. Instagram handle and phone line, if the studio wants them public.
