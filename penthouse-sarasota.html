Project 04 of 06

A penthouse above downtown Sarasota, designed around the view and a disciplined palette of black, white and pale oak.

The kitchen is the boldest move: black cabinetry and a long island under a row of pendants, set against pale floors so the room stays light. A powder room wrapped in botanical wallpaper is the one place the home raises its voice, a floating stair lets daylight fall through the middle of the plan, and the bedrooms soften into linen and texture.

The project was featured in Florida Design.

Black

Used where hands touch, on cabinetry, table and hardware, so it reads as craft rather than drama.

Pattern

One room is allowed to be loud, so the rest of the home can stay calm.

Light

An open stair and pale oak keep daylight moving through the middle of the home.
