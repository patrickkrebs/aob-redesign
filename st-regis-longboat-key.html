Resort living on the Gulf. Residences and amenity spaces for the St. Regis Longboat Key, where coming home should feel like checking in.

The palette is sand, linen and pale stone, warmed by brass and cove lighting so evenings glow rather than glare. Arched niches and curved seating soften the architecture, and every terrace is furnished as a room of its own.

Images shown are design visualizations of the residences and amenity spaces.
