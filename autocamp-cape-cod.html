Project 02 of 06

The brief was a clubhouse that would feel like the living room of the Cape: somewhere to gather by a fire after a day on the water, with the pines always in view.

The studio answered with a few honest materials used generously. Warm timber lines the walls and ceiling, glass opens the room to the forest, and a suspended fireplace anchors a sunken lounge at its center. Low, deep seating invites guests to stay, and every surface is chosen to wear in rather than wear out.

The result received the Interior Design award for hospitality.

Timber

One warm wood, used on walls, ceiling and millwork, so the room reads as a single crafted object.

Fire

A suspended hearth that can be seen from every seat, giving the open plan a center.

Forest

Glazing set low and wide so the pines frame every view, by day and at blue hour.
