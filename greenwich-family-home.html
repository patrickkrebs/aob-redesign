A family home in Greenwich with steel windows, a wall of books and a breakfast nook that catches the morning.

The house is generous, so the studio worked to make it feel personal room by room. Soft upholstery and walnut sit against black steel windows in the living room, the library is lined floor to ceiling, and the breakfast nook is built in under a cluster of glass pendants.
